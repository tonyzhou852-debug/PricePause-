About us

Our story

PricePause started with a simple observation: almost everyone has bought something in a hurry, then found it cheaper five minutes later. We wanted a tool that shows up at exactly that moment. It isn't there to nag you, just to give you a calm second thought.

It began as a school project, and we built a working Chrome extension that spots the product you're looking at, pulls live prices from Google Shopping, and lines up coupons so you can compare in one click. The coupon feature is still a prototype, and we're improving it.

Our mission: we help impulsive buyers spend smarter by pausing them before checkout and surfacing better prices and coupons.

Questions, answered

Is PricePause free?

Yes to start. The free plan includes 15 live price searches a month. Premium gives you unlimited searches for $4.99/month or $39.99/year.

Where do the prices come from?

Live comparisons come from Google Shopping, so you're seeing what other retailers are actually listing right now.

Which stores does it work on?

It detects products automatically on Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Etsy, eBay and Shopify stores. You can also type any product name into the search box on any site.

What does it do with my data?

It reads the product name and price on the page you're viewing and uses the product name to look up prices. Your savings total and monthly search count stay in your own browser.

When can I get it?
